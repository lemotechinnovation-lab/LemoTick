# South African Regulatory Requirements for LemoTick Investment Platform

## 📋 **Executive Summary**

This document outlines the comprehensive regulatory requirements for transforming LemoTick from an educational trading bot into a commercial investment platform in South Africa. The regulatory landscape is governed by multiple acts and requires careful compliance with the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA).

---

## 🏛️ **Primary Regulatory Framework**

### **1. Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA)**
- **Main Regulator**: Financial services licensing and oversight
- **Website**: https://www.fsca.co.za
- **Contact**: +27 12 428 8000

### **2. Key Regulatory Acts**
- **FAIS Act**: Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act
- **FICA**: Financial Intelligence Centre Act (AML/CTF)
- **POPIA**: Protection of Personal Information Act
- **COFI Bill**: Conduct of Financial Institutions (upcoming)

---

## 🎯 **Required Licenses for LemoTick**

### **Primary License: FSP (Financial Services Provider)**

#### **Category II FSP License** (Recommended for LemoTick)
- **Purpose**: Discretionary portfolio management
- **Scope**: Automated trading with client fund discretion
- **Requirements**: Most comprehensive compliance obligations

#### **Alternative Categories:**
- **Category I**: Advisory services only (no discretion)
- **Category IIA**: Hedge fund management
- **Category III**: Administrative services

---

## 📝 **FSP License Application Requirements**

### **1. Company Structure Requirements**

#### **Minimum Personnel:**
- **Shareholders**: At least 1 shareholder
- **Directors**: At least 1 director
- **Key Individual**: 1 South African resident (mandatory)
- **Compliance Officer**: 1 independent compliance officer

#### **Key Individual Requirements:**
- **Residency**: Must be South African resident
- **Qualifications**: Relevant financial services experience
- **Examinations**: Must pass RE1 and RE5 regulatory exams
- **Fit and Proper**: Honesty, integrity, competence assessment

### **2. Capital Requirements**

#### **Capital Adequacy:**
- **Minimum**: Liquid assets ≥ 4/52 weeks of annual expenditure
- **Calculation**: Based on operating expenses and liabilities
- **Maintenance**: Ongoing capital adequacy monitoring required
- **Reporting**: Quarterly capital adequacy reports to FSCA

#### **Professional Indemnity Insurance:**
- **Coverage**: Professional negligence claims
- **Amount**: Varies by business size and risk profile
- **Provider**: Must be FSCA-approved insurer
- **Renewal**: Annual renewal required

### **3. Regulatory Examinations**

#### **Required Exams:**
- **RE1 (Regulatory Exam 1)**: Regulatory framework and responsibilities
- **RE5 (Regulatory Exam 5)**: Financial services representatives
- **Passing Score**: 65% minimum
- **Validity**: 3 years (renewal required)

#### **Exam Content:**
- **FAIS Act**: Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services
- **FICA**: Anti-money laundering legislation
- **FSCA Rules**: Conduct of business rules
- **Ethics**: Professional conduct and ethics

## 🔒 **Anti-Money Laundering (FICA) Compliance**

### **Critical Requirements (FATF Grey List Impact)**

#### **1. Accountable Institution Status**
- **Classification**: FSPs are accountable institutions under FICA
- **Obligations**: Enhanced AML/CTF requirements
- **Penalties**: Severe penalties for non-compliance

#### **2. Client Due Diligence (CDD)**
- **KYC Procedures**: Know Your Customer requirements
- **Identity Verification**: Government-issued ID verification
- **Address Verification**: Proof of residence
- **Beneficial Ownership**: Ultimate beneficial owner identification

#### **3. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**
- **High-Risk Clients**: Politically exposed persons (PEPs)
- **High-Risk Countries**: Enhanced screening for grey-listed countries
- **Large Transactions**: Additional verification for large amounts
- **Ongoing Monitoring**: Continuous client risk assessment

#### **4. Reporting Obligations**
- **Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs)**: Report to FIC
- **Cash Transaction Reports (CTRs)**: Large cash transactions
- **Terrorist Financing Reports**: Terrorist financing suspicions
- **Record Keeping**: 5-year record retention

### **5. AML/CTF Policies Required**
- **Risk Assessment**: Comprehensive risk assessment framework
- **Training Programs**: Staff AML/CTF training
- **Monitoring Systems**: Transaction monitoring systems
- **Reporting Procedures**: Internal reporting procedures
